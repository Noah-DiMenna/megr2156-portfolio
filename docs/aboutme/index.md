## What does it mean to defend an engineering decision : and do you currently know how to do it?
defending an engineering decision is the act of supplying information and proper data to defend why any particular choice made by an engineer was correct. This could be a design choice, material choice, or anything an engineer has to decide when going through research and development in their field. Defending an engineers decision is more than just claiming it is the best, it required theorizing any alternatives and testing both to see which outcome is best. There has to be data to support the decision, whether that data was obtained from another credible source, or testing done by the engineer making the decision. I currently do not know every detail when it comes to defending the decisions of an engineer, but I do know that lots of data, tests, and research are needed if the engineer wants their decision to be the right one.
