## Task A:
**Portfolio 1: [Alexandre Allonas Portfolio](https://www.alexandre-allonas.fr)**
a. Navigability
Alexandre Allonas has a portfolio that is well structured overall. It clearly states his name at the top, along with where he is a student. It drops you right into a personal information page so you can get to know the person the portfolio belongs to. Just below his name there are easy to see buttons that take you to other parts of the portfolio, like projects, experience, and a contact page. It is easy to locate the projects that Alexandre has worked on in the past under the projects button, and it loads very fast. Each project has a quick snapshot of what was included with key features and a picture as well. Some project have buttons that take you to additional information where they were needed. 

b. Reproducibility
This portfolio does not seem as though it was intended as a way to recreate the projects he has listed. Rather so, it is an exposition of the projects he has worked on in the past. So recreating the work done from Alexandre would be near to impossible without asking any questions, because not much more than the key details of any given project are presented to us. The only workaround to this would be that Alexandre gave multiple forms of contact information that could be used to obtain more information regarding each project. 

c. Evidence of reasoning
This portfolio does not show how many different decisions were made in any of the projects, it mainly just shows the final designs for each project, as well as key features that go into it. For example, the VTOL project does not show any information other than the key features like: 3m wingspan, 500g payload, and 30 minute flight time. There is no information on what changes were made to the design throughout the portfolio. The video that goes along with this project is just a test flight, so no new information about design changes can be obtained from this either. 
d.  Professional tone
Overall this portfolio maintains a professional tone with what is included for the viewer. The about section follows this with fully formatted paragraphs rather than making bullet points or any other form of list. But it lacks when coming to the overall information that is shown to us about the projects he contributed towards.
